## Master Positioning Statement
OnlineCheckWriter.com is the all-in-one payment platform for small businesses that still need checks — letting them print, mail, or send any payment from a single login, without pre-printed stock, without re-entry, and without the fraud exposure of a traditional paper process. It doesn't ask businesses to stop using checks. It makes checks effortless.

## Tone of Voice

### How We Sound
- **Practical and plain** — No jargon. No startup language. Speak the way a sharp accountant explains something to a client: clearly, quickly, and with the dollar figure up front.
- **Professional, not corporate** — Authoritative and trustworthy without being stiff or formal.
- **Pain-point led** — Lead with the problem the user already feels, then deliver the specific relief.
- **Confident, not hypey** — State outcomes with conviction. Let proof points do the selling.

### What NOT to Say
- "Revolutionary payment solution"
- "AI-powered" (without specific, meaningful context)
- "Disrupting the check industry" — this audience doesn't identify as early adopters
- Feature lists without outcomes — "MICR ink support" means nothing; "bank-accepted checks" does
- Startup or VC-speak of any kind

### Approved Copy Patterns
| Situation | Formula |
|---|---|
| Search ads | Lead with the instant relief — "Print Business Checks Instantly — No Pre-Printed Stock Needed" |
| Homepage hero | All payment methods, single login — "Pay Any Way. One Platform." |
| Security | Name the certification — "SOC2 Type II. PCI-DSS. Your money stays yours." |
| Time savings | Make it visceral — "Cut check day from 3 hours to 15 minutes" |
| Cost savings | Make it concrete — "Save up to $1,400/year vs. pre-printed stock" |
| CPA audience | Multiplier framing — "Manage check payments for all your clients from one dashboard" |
| Retargeting | Price objection head-on — "Stop paying $80 a box for pre-printed checks." |
